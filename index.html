Rose,

If you’re reading this, you opened it on your own, and that already means a lot to me.

I wanted you to have something that doesn’t depend on a phone call, a good day, or the right timing. Something you can come back to whenever you want, like a letter tucked in a notebook.

I love you. I loved you with my whole heart, and no amount of time changes that.

You made ordinary days feel like they were worth remembering. You made me want to be better, softer, and more patient. Whatever happened, whatever is still unsaid, that part was real.

I’m not writing this to pull you back or to ask you for anything. I’m writing it so you always know the truth: you were loved, truly loved, by me.

Wherever life takes you, I hope it’s gentle. I hope you laugh a lot, I hope you rest when you’re tired, and I hope you never doubt how much you’re worth.

Always yours,
Martin
